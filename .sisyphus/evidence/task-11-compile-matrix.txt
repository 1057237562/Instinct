observations:
  * compile cuts Mode-0 peak 36% (11445 -> 7301 MB) — Triton fusion shrinks
    intermediate activations in backward — and speeds it up 2.6x.
  * Mode 2 (whole-layer torch.utils.checkpoint) is the big compile winner:
    peak 3431 -> 2105 MB (-38% vs eager M2) and 2.500 -> 7.347 steps/s (2.9x),
    because the tiny per-block recompute graphs fuse well.
  * Mode 1 (selective attention recompute) sees the least compile benefit
    (2.792 -> 3.205 steps/s, +15%): RecomputeAttention keeps graph breaks in the
    hot path, as hypothesized — checkpoint regions act as compile graph boundaries.
  * compile also lifts the best-case memory: mode2-compile 2104.8 MB vs
    mode2-eager 3430.6 MB (-38.6%).
